Most issues with Orbit AI have a straightforward fix. This guide walks you through the most common problems reported across the workspace, API, browser extension, and desktop app — with step-by-step resolutions for each. Work through the relevant section below, and if the problem persists, contact the Orbit Labs support team at [https://www.orbitdev.org](https://www.orbitdev.org).

<Tip>
  Before digging into a specific section, try a hard refresh (`Ctrl + Shift + R` on Windows/Linux, `Cmd + Shift + R` on macOS) and sign out then back in. These two steps resolve a surprising number of transient issues.
</Tip>

## Workspace Issues

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="Can't sign in to my account">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Double-check your credentials">
        Confirm you are using the email address associated with your Orbit AI account and that Caps Lock is off when entering your password.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Reset your password">
        On the [sign-in page](https://app.orbitdev.org/auth), click **Forgot password?** and follow the instructions sent to your email. Check your spam folder if the email does not arrive within a few minutes.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Clear browser cookies">
        Stale session cookies can block the sign-in flow. Open your browser settings, clear cookies for `app.orbitdev.org`, and try signing in again.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Try a different browser or private window">
        Open a private/incognito window or a different browser to rule out browser-extension conflicts interfering with the sign-in page.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
      If your organisation uses SAML SSO (Enterprise plan), use the **Sign in with SSO** option on the sign-in page and enter your company email domain. Contact your IT administrator if SSO is not working.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Workspace not loading or showing a blank screen">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Check your internet connection">
        Open another website to confirm you have a working connection. Orbit AI requires a stable internet connection for all AI features.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Try a different browser">
        Orbit AI is tested on the latest versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox, and Safari. If you are using an older browser version, update it or switch to a supported one.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Disable browser extensions">
        Some ad blockers and security extensions intercept requests to `api.orbitdev.org`. Temporarily disable extensions and reload the page to see if one is causing the issue.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Clear cache and hard-refresh">
        Press `Ctrl + Shift + R` (Windows/Linux) or `Cmd + Shift + R` (macOS) to force a full reload without the cache.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## API Issues

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="401 Unauthorized">
    Your request does not include a valid API key, or the key has been revoked.

    **Check the following:**

    - Confirm the `Authorization` header is present and formatted correctly:
      ```bash
      Authorization: Bearer orb_your_api_key
      ```
    - Verify that the key starts with `orb_` and has not been truncated or wrapped with extra whitespace.
    - Open the [developer dashboard](https://app.orbitdev.org/developer) and confirm the key is listed and not marked as revoked.
    - If you recently rotated keys, make sure your code is using the new key and not a cached older one.

    <Warning>
      If your key was accidentally committed to a public repository, revoke it immediately from the [developer dashboard](https://app.orbitdev.org/developer) and generate a new one. Treat all previously exposed keys as compromised.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="402 Payment Required">
    Your account does not have sufficient credits or you have exceeded your plan's task limit.

    **Resolution options:**

    - Log in and go to [app.orbitdev.org/billing](https://app.orbitdev.org/billing) to review your current usage and upgrade your plan.
    - If you are on the free Developer plan (1,000 tasks/month), consider upgrading to **Orbit Pro** (€10/month) or purchasing the paid API tier (€10 one-time for 10,000 requests/month).
    - If you are on the free API tier (100 requests/month), upgrade to the paid API tier from the [developer dashboard](https://app.orbitdev.org/developer).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="422 Unprocessable Entity">
    The server understood your request but could not process it because the request body is malformed or missing required fields.

    **Common causes and fixes:**

    - **Missing required fields** — check the API reference for the endpoint you are calling and ensure all required parameters are included.
    - **Wrong content type** — make sure you are sending `Content-Type: application/json` and that your body is valid JSON.
    - **Invalid field values** — for example, passing a string where a number is expected, or an unsupported value for an enum field.

    Use a tool like `jq` or an online JSON validator to check your request body before sending:

    ```bash
    echo '{"model":"auto","messages":[{"role":"user","content":"Hello"}]}' | jq .
    ```
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="429 Too Many Requests">
    You have exceeded the rate limit for your API tier.

    | Tier | Rate limit | Monthly quota |
    |------|-----------|---------------|
    | Free | 5 req/min | 100 req/month |
    | Paid | 30 req/min | 10,000 req/month |

    **How to handle this:**

    - Read the `Retry-After` header in the response — it tells you how many seconds to wait before retrying.
    - Implement **exponential backoff** in your code: wait 1 second, then 2, then 4, doubling after each failed retry up to a maximum delay.
    - If you consistently hit rate limits, upgrade to the paid API tier from the [developer dashboard](https://app.orbitdev.org/developer).

    ```javascript
    async function fetchWithRetry(url, options, retries = 3) {
      for (let i = 0; i < retries; i++) {
        const res = await fetch(url, options);
        if (res.status !== 429) return res;
        const retryAfter = res.headers.get("Retry-After") || Math.pow(2, i);
        await new Promise((r) => setTimeout(r, retryAfter * 1000));
      }
      throw new Error("Rate limit exceeded after retries");
    }
    ```
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="500 Internal Server Error">
    An unexpected error occurred on the Orbit AI server side. This is not caused by your request.

    **Steps to take:**

    1. Wait a few seconds and retry the request — transient server errors typically resolve quickly.
    2. If the error persists for more than a few minutes, check the [Orbit Labs status page](https://www.orbitdev.org) for any active incidents.
    3. If no incident is reported and the error continues, contact support with the timestamp of your request, the endpoint you called, and the full response body.

    <Note>
      Your monthly quota is not decremented for requests that result in a `5xx` error. You are only charged for requests that return a successful `2xx` response.
    </Note>
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Browser Extension Issues

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="The Orbit AI panel is not appearing on pages">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Sign in to the web app first">
        The browser extension requires an active session at [app.orbitdev.org](https://app.orbitdev.org). Open the web app in a separate tab, sign in, then return to the page where you want to use the extension.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check the extension is enabled for the site">
        Click the puzzle-piece icon in your browser toolbar, find the Orbit AI extension, and confirm it is set to **Allow on all sites** or is specifically allowed on the current domain.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Reload the extension">
        Go to your browser's extensions page (`chrome://extensions` in Chrome), toggle the Orbit AI extension off and back on, then reload the page.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Page context is not loading in the extension panel">
    If the extension opens but shows an error loading the page context:

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Refresh the page">
        Close the Orbit AI panel, press `F5` to reload the page fully, then re-open the panel.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check for content security policy restrictions">
        Some websites use strict Content Security Policies (CSP) that prevent extensions from reading page content. On these sites, Orbit AI can still run as a standalone panel but cannot access the page context. This is expected behaviour and not a bug.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Update the extension">
        Make sure you are running the latest version of the Orbit AI extension. Visit the Chrome Web Store or Edge Add-ons page and check for updates.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>

## Desktop App Issues

<AccordionGroup>

  <Accordion title="The desktop app won't launch">
    <Steps>
      <Step title="Confirm you are running Windows">
        The Orbit AI desktop app currently supports **Windows only**. macOS and Linux users should access the platform at [app.orbitdev.org](https://app.orbitdev.org) in their browser.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Reinstall the application">
        Download the latest installer from [app.orbitdev.org](https://app.orbitdev.org), uninstall the existing version from **Settings → Apps → Installed Apps**, then run the new installer.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Check Windows event logs">
        If reinstalling does not help, open **Event Viewer** (`Win + R`, type `eventvwr`), navigate to **Windows Logs → Application**, and look for recent errors from the Orbit AI process. Share these details with support if you need further assistance.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Warning>
      Do not run multiple instances of the Orbit AI desktop app simultaneously. If the app appears frozen, end the process in **Task Manager** before attempting to relaunch.
    </Warning>
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Not signed in after installing the desktop app">
    The desktop app does not carry over browser sessions — you need to sign in separately after installation.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Open the desktop app">
        Launch the Orbit AI app from the Start menu or desktop shortcut.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Sign in with your Orbit account">
        On the sign-in screen, enter the email address and password associated with your Orbit AI account. If your organisation uses SSO, click **Sign in with SSO** and enter your company domain.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Stay signed in">
        Check **Keep me signed in** on the sign-in screen to avoid being prompted for credentials on every launch.
      </Step>
    </Steps>
  </Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
