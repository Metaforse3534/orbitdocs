Orbit AI uses a secure email-and-password authentication system to protect your workspace, data, and API credentials. Whether you are creating a new account or returning to an existing one, everything starts at [app.orbitdev.org/auth](https://app.orbitdev.org/auth). Once signed in, you can manage all account details — including your profile, billing, and connected integrations — from the central settings page.

## Create an Account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the sign-up page">
    Navigate to [https://app.orbitdev.org/auth](https://app.orbitdev.org/auth) in your browser.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Provide your name, email address, and a strong password. Read and accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy before continuing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from Orbit Labs. Click the confirmation link to activate your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access your workspace">
    After verification, you are redirected to your Orbit AI workspace at [https://app.orbitdev.org](https://app.orbitdev.org) and can start using the platform immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Sign In

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-in page">
    Go to [https://app.orbitdev.org/auth](https://app.orbitdev.org/auth).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Type your registered email address and password, then click **Sign In**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reset your password if needed">
    If you cannot remember your password, click **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page and follow the email reset flow.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage Your Account

All authentication-related settings live at [https://app.orbitdev.org/settings](https://app.orbitdev.org/settings). From there you can:

- **Update your email address** — enter a new address and confirm the change via a verification link sent to your new email.
- **Change your password** — enter your current password and choose a new one. Changes take effect immediately and invalidate any active sessions on other devices.
- **Review active sessions** — see where your account is currently signed in and revoke any sessions you do not recognise.

## Password Security Best Practices

Follow these guidelines to keep your Orbit AI account secure:

- Use a password that is at least 12 characters long and includes a mix of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols.
- Never reuse a password you have used on another service.
- Store your password in a dedicated password manager rather than in a browser or a plain-text file.
- Change your password immediately if you suspect your credentials have been compromised.
- Sign out of shared or public computers after every session.

<Note>
  **Enterprise SSO:** Enterprise plans support SAML 2.0 and SCIM provisioning for single sign-on (SSO) and automated user lifecycle management. Contact the Orbit Labs sales team at [orbitdev.org](https://www.orbitdev.org) to enable SSO for your organisation.
</Note>

## API Authentication

If you are building integrations or using the Orbit AI REST API, you authenticate with API keys rather than your email and password. See [API Keys](/account/api-keys) for full instructions on generating, using, and revoking keys.
