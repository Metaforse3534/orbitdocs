Orbit AI is an intelligent workspace built by Orbit Labs, a Netherlands-based company, for professionals, developers, researchers, and teams who need capable AI that they can actually trust. Rather than scattering your work across a collection of disconnected tools, Orbit brings AI chat, autonomous agents, workflow automation, file analysis, voice workflows, artifact creation, and a full developer API into a single, coherent environment — designed from the ground up to be GDPR-compliant, transparent about what it does, and honest about what it doesn't.

## What Orbit AI gives you

Orbit is not a single-purpose AI chatbot. It is a full workspace that grows with the complexity of your work. Here is what is available today:

| Capability | Description |
|---|---|
| **AI Chat** | Conversational AI for questions, writing, reasoning, and analysis — powered by leading AI models. |
| **AI Agents** | Autonomous agents (Lead, Builder, Researcher, Reviewer) that decompose goals, parallelize tool calls, and converge on verified results. |
| **Workflow Automation** | Multi-step, automated pipelines that connect AI reasoning to real-world actions without manual intervention. |
| **Files & Research** | Deep reading and synthesis of uploaded documents, datasets, and information sources. |
| **Voice Workflows** | Voice-driven interaction for hands-free productivity and dictation-style task execution. |
| **Artifact Creation** | Generate structured outputs — reports, code, summaries, and documents — that are ready to use. |
| **Windows Desktop App** | A native Windows application for a local-first, distraction-free AI workspace experience. |
| **Browser Extension** | Bring Orbit's AI capabilities directly into your browser for in-context assistance wherever you work. |
| **Public REST API** | Integrate Orbit intelligence into your own applications with a documented, versioned REST API. |

## Who Orbit is built for

Orbit is designed for people who rely on AI for real work — not just occasional curiosity.

- **Developers** who want to integrate AI chat, agent runs, and usage tracking directly into their applications via a clean REST API.
- **Researchers** who need to ingest, synthesize, and reason over large volumes of documents and sources efficiently.
- **Professionals** who want a single workspace for writing, analysis, automation, and decision support without switching between a dozen tools.
- **Teams** who need shared context, collaborative workflows, and centralized administration without sacrificing privacy or auditability.

## How Orbit is different

Most AI tools are optimized to be impressive. Orbit is optimized to be dependable.

**Privacy is an engineering constraint, not a marketing claim.** Orbit does not collect your data for advertising. Your conversations and files are not used to train models without your consent. Orbit is operated under Dutch and European law, complies with GDPR, and maintains a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) for business customers.

**Orbit tells you what it knows and what it doesn't.** Capabilities are labeled with their actual status. The documentation describes what works today — not a roadmap dressed up as a product.

**Everything in one workspace.** Instead of maintaining subscriptions to a chat tool, a research tool, an automation tool, and a coding assistant, Orbit connects all of these in a single environment built around how knowledge work actually flows.

**European hosting and data protection.** Orbit Labs is based in the Netherlands. Your data is handled under some of the strongest privacy regulations in the world — by design, not by accident.
