The Orbit AI desktop app is a native Windows application that extends your Orbit workspace beyond the browser. While the web workspace handles chat, agents, and cloud-based automation, the desktop app adds a direct bridge between Orbit's AI capabilities and your local machine — letting you run creation workflows that read from and write to your own files, automate desktop-level actions, and access certain features even when your internet connection is limited. It is the same Orbit workspace you already know, with a deeper reach into your environment.

<Note>
  The Orbit desktop app is available on the **Orbit Pro** plan and above. Sign in to your account and confirm your plan at [app.orbitdev.org](https://app.orbitdev.org) before downloading.
</Note>

## How to Install

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to your Orbit Pro account">
    Open [app.orbitdev.org](https://app.orbitdev.org) in your browser and sign in. Confirm that your account is on the Orbit Pro plan or above — the download is unlocked for Pro and Team subscribers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to the download section">
    Once signed in, locate the desktop app download section within the app dashboard or your account settings. The current Windows installer will be listed there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download and run the Windows installer">
    Download the `.exe` installer file and run it on your Windows machine. Follow the on-screen prompts to complete installation. The app installs to your system and creates a desktop shortcut.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with your Orbit account">
    Launch the Orbit desktop app from your desktop or Start menu. Sign in using your existing Orbit credentials. Your workspace, agents, and settings sync automatically from your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Local Creation Workflows in Practice

Local creation workflows are one of the most powerful features exclusive to the desktop app. You point Orbit at a folder or file on your machine, define the task — rewrite these drafts, summarize these logs, generate code from these specs — and Orbit reads the input, processes it, and writes the result back to your chosen local destination.

This means large batches of files can be processed without manual uploading, and outputs land exactly where your existing tools expect them, ready for immediate use.

<Tip>
  Combine local creation workflows with Orbit's **Workflow Automation** feature to build hands-free pipelines that run on a schedule or trigger. For example, set up a workflow that picks up new files dropped into a folder, summarizes them with the Researcher agent, and saves structured reports to an output directory — all without manual intervention.
</Tip>
