Orbit Extension Pro is a browser companion that brings the full intelligence of your Orbit AI workspace into every page you visit — without switching tabs or copying content manually. It connects to your active Orbit account and surfaces three core capabilities directly in your browser: reading and reasoning about the current page, working with text you highlight, and drafting form suggestions for your review. Everything stays under your control and is tied to your subscription.

## Get the Extension

Download and manage Orbit Extension Pro from the dedicated extension hub inside the Orbit app:

**[app.orbitdev.org/extension](https://app.orbitdev.org/extension)**

<Note>
  Orbit Extension Pro is available on the **Orbit Pro** plan and above. If you are on the free Developer plan, upgrade your plan at [app.orbitdev.org](https://app.orbitdev.org) to unlock the extension.
</Note>

## Key Capabilities

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Page Context" icon="browser">
    Ask questions about the page you are currently viewing. Orbit scans the readable content of the page and uses it as context for your query — no copy-paste required.

  <Card title="Selected Text" icon="text-slash">
    Highlight any text on any page and invoke Orbit to summarize, explain, rewrite, or expand it. The selection is sent directly to your Orbit session without leaving the page.

  <Card title="Review-First Form Fill" icon="pen-to-square">

## How to Install

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Extension Hub">
    Go to [app.orbitdev.org/extension](https://app.orbitdev.org/extension) in your browser. Sign in to your Orbit account if prompted.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download for your browser">
    Select your browser from the available options and follow the download prompt to add the extension.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with your Orbit account">
    After installation, the extension will prompt you to authenticate. Sign in using the same credentials you use for the Orbit workspace at [app.orbitdev.org](https://app.orbitdev.org).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the toolbar icon">
    Once signed in, the Orbit extension icon appears in your browser toolbar. A green "connected" indicator confirms the extension is active and linked to your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Using the Extension

### Opening the Orbit Panel

Click the Orbit icon in your browser toolbar at any time to open the Orbit panel. From here you can type a prompt, view your recent sessions, and access workspace settings — all without leaving the current page.

### Reading Page Content

Navigate to any page with readable content. The extension automatically makes the current page available as context. In the Orbit panel, ask questions about what is on the page — Orbit reads and reasons over the content in real time.

### Working With Selected Text

Highlight any passage of text on a page, then right-click to reveal Orbit options in the context menu. Choose from actions such as **Summarize**, **Explain**, or **Expand** to send the selection directly to Orbit and see a response in the panel.

<Tip>
  Use the selection workflow when you encounter a technical term, a lengthy clause in a document, or any passage you want clarified without navigating away from the page.
</Tip>

### Review-First Form Fill

When you focus on a form field that Orbit can suggest a completion for, a subtle Orbit prompt appears. Click it to see the suggested draft. Review it carefully — the text is never entered automatically.

<Warning>
  Always read and verify any form fill suggestion produced by Orbit before accepting it. This is especially important for fields involving personal information, financial data, legal content, or any submission that cannot easily be undone. Orbit's review-first design means nothing is submitted on your behalf — the final action is always yours.
</Warning>
