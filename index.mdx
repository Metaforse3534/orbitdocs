## Get up and running in three steps

Start using Orbit AI right away — no prior setup or technical knowledge required.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your free account">
    Sign up at [app.orbitdev.org/auth](https://app.orbitdev.org/auth) using your email address. The Developer plan is free and requires no credit card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the workspace or grab your API key">
    Once logged in, launch the AI workspace directly in your browser to start chatting, uploading files, or running agents. If you want programmatic access, navigate to [app.orbitdev.org/developer](https://app.orbitdev.org/developer) to generate your API key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start working with AI">
    Send your first message in the chat interface, trigger an agent workflow, or call `POST /v1/chat/completions` from your own application. Orbit handles the rest — privately, reliably, and under your control.
  </Step>
</Steps>
